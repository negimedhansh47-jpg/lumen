How it teaches

Not just documentation. A tutor that points at your own code.

  1. Grounded in evidenceEvery claim about a repo cites a real file and lines. If Lumen cannot find evidence, it says less.
  2. At your levelBeginner explains every term. Intermediate adds trade-offs and alternatives.
  3. Concepts you can openTap any concept chip for a short card: what it is, an analogy, when to use it, and the trade-offs.

Your chats, ideas and private code are processed in memory and never stored. Only analyses of public repositories are cached.
