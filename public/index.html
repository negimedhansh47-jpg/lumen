How it teaches

Not documentation. A tutor that points at your own code.

  1. Grounded in evidenceEvery claim about a repo cites a real file and lines. If Lumen cannot find evidence, it says less.
  2. At your levelBeginner explains every term. Intermediate adds trade-offs and alternatives.
  3. Concepts you can openTap any concept chip for a short card, and ask for a different explanation if it does not click.

Your chats, ideas and private code are processed in memory and never stored. Only analyses of public repositories are cached. Your saved project lives only in your own browser.
