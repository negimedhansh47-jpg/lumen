Why you can trust it

Not generic tutorials. A tutor that points at your own code.

  1. Shows its evidenceEvery claim about a repo names a real file. If Lumen cannot find evidence, it says less.
  2. At your levelPick "Beginners" for plain words, or "Some experience" for trade-offs and alternatives.
  3. Click any blue ideaBlue chips open a short explanation. If it does not click, ask for it explained another way.

Your chats, ideas and private code are processed in memory and never stored. Only analyses of public repositories are cached. Your saved project lives only in your own browser.
